00 — Set up your laboratory

Start a new project, name it FRACTURE ENGINE, set tempo to 140 BPM, and use a straight groove of 6 ticks per row. Reserve tracks 1–8 for kick, snare, response, hats, open hats, micro percussion, glitch and control.

01 — FM percussion workshop

Manual reference: April 2026 M8 Operation Manual, pp. 18–21, 58–60. These are starting patches, not hardware-tested presets. All two-digit settings are hex; ratios are decimal.

Routing clarification: On Instrument View, the FM macro sliders above the filter are MOD1–MOD4 base values. Under each operator, a MOD slot assigns a macro to LEV/RAT/PIT/FBK. On the separate Modulation View, an AHD source must have DEST set to the intended FM macro (e.g., MOD 3) to reach that operator slot. DEST PITCH instead changes overall instrument pitch. Keep original patches if you prefer their sound.

01A — Timbre Contrast Addendum · five new FM voices

Preserve instruments 00–06. Build these additional voices in 07–0B. Their purpose is to make probability, microtiming, polymeter and event relationships easier to hear. These are unverified starting patches, not exact replicas of particular acoustic instruments. Test and tune them on your M8. The original tracks still use 00–06 until you intentionally substitute a new instrument.
