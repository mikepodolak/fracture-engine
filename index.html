FRACTURE ENGINE

A hands-on percussion cookbook.

00 — Set up your laboratory

Start a new project, name it FRACTURE ENGINE, set tempo to 140 BPM, and use a straight groove of 6 ticks per row. Reserve tracks 1–8 for kick, snare, response, hats, open hats, micro percussion, glitch and control.

00.01 — Establish a baseline

Load a known working percussion instrument into instrument 00. Create phrase 00 on track 1 and enter the following hits. Leave all other rows and FX empty. Loop the phrase.

ROW NOTE VEL INS FX1 FX2 FX3
00  C-2  70  00  --- --- ---
04  C-2  60  00  --- --- ---
08  C-2  70  00  --- --- ---
0C  C-2  60  00  --- --- ---

Listen for four evenly spaced hits. If silent, check the instrument, track mute, and note range. The note is a starting point, not a universal drum sample mapping.

Remaining chapters

Euclidean rhythm lab

Distribute pulses evenly across steps. Rotate to shift the starting point. This generates a note grid, not an M8 project file.

For fewer than 16 steps, the grid alone does not shorten an M8 phrase. Configure the phrase boundary separately.
